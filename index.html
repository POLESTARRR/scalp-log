    Malassezia has no fatty acid synthase gene — it cannot build the lipids it needs, so it secretes lipases and scavenges them off your skin, using chains roughly C11 to C24. A conditioner built on coconut oil is therefore feeding the exact yeast your shampoo is trying to kill. Paste any ingredient list; it never leaves this device.
